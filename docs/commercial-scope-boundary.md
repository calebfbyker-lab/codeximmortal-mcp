# Commercial channel scope boundary

codeximmortal.com serves human customers. The deployed codeximmortal-mcp service
serves external agents; the repository publishes code, catalogs and discovery.
Agents must not need to navigate the human storefront to purchase an agent-supported
offer. This is target architecture, not proof that purchasing is operational.

The initial new services are Pricing Snapshot, Pricing Change Comparison and
Structured Document Extraction. Existing storefront products and Web-to-JSON bundles
are not silently substituted for these services or their entitlements.

War Lab-specific OODA presentation, CI allocation, Shield Score, AX labels and defense
approval conditions are not commercial release requirements. Security, privacy,
legal obligations, explicit buyer spending authorization, owner deployment approval
and accurate claims remain required. This file cannot override platform policies or
instructions configured outside this repository.

Base44 remains the sole authority for buyers, entitlements, balances, jobs, results
and settlement unless the owner separately approves a migration. Channel separation
is not approval for a competing financial ledger or live payment activation.
Buyer-assisted checkout and delegated machine purchasing are distinct capabilities.
A catalog listing or hosted checkout URL does not demonstrate autonomous purchasing.
